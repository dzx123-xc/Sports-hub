The Trust & Discovery Principle

"Talent should be discoverable, not just famous." Here is how SportsConnect protects data credibility:

1️⃣ Structured Skill Tests

Sport-specific assessments (Batting accuracy, reaction time, speed, agility) recorded by certified evaluators.

2️⃣ Authorized Match Records

Match statistics are submitted exclusively by tournament organizers and clubs, never self-declared by athletes.

3️⃣ Dynamic Classification

Our weighted algorithm evaluates skill (30%), match performance (30%), and progress (20%), ensuring experience does not dominate.

4️⃣ Admin Investigation

Any disputed credential undergoes multi-stage verification before approval or disciplinary action is taken.
